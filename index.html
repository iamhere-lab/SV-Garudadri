About SV Garudadri

S.V. Sai Builders introduces SV Garudadri — a rich gated community at ORR Exit-9, Ghatkesar, with two independent towers of 135 lavish flats, five floors each.

Life is not only about living in a comfortable flat. That is why the community comes with a club house equipped with a super market, business lounge, library, gym, indoor games, multi-purpose hall and guest rooms — along with a kids' play area, open gym, walking track around the community and a meeting point for senior citizens.

A dedicated EV charging point, a swimming pool and a unique temple within the premises complete a home that is built for today and ready for tomorrow.

Specifications

Structure

Reinforced cement concrete framed structure.

Super Structure

Main Door

Hard wood frame with designed paneled shutters, S.S. coated brass fixtures and reputed make lock.

Internal Doors

Hard wood frame with flush door shutters, synthetic enamel paint and S.S. coated fixtures.

Windows

UPVC sliding shutters with mosquito shutter provision, plain glass and safety grills.

Flooring

2' × 2' vitrified tiles for the entire flat and corridor.

Internal Finish

Two-coat cement plastering with smooth lappam finish, painted with emulsion.

External Finish

Two-coat cement plastering painted with waterproof cement paint of reputed make.

Electrical

Concealed copper wiring with adequate points and modular fixtures of reputed make.

Water Supply & Drainage

C.P.V.C. water lines with C.P. fixtures; P.V.C. lines for drainage and rain water.

Sanitary & Cladding

Ceramic sanitary fixtures; door-height glazed tile dado for toilet walls.

Lifts & Generator

Six passenger lifts; back-up power for lifts, water pumping motors and common lighting.

FAQs

Where exactly is SV Garudadri located?

SV Garudadri is at Survey No. 90/4, near Rockwood International School, Yamnampet, Ghatkesar Mandal, Medchal–Malkajgiri District, Telangana 501301 — about 2 minutes from ORR Exit-9.

What configurations are available?

2 BHK homes from 927 to 1,297 sq.ft and 3 BHK homes from 1,350 to 1,720 sq.ft, in East, West and North facing options.

How many flats are there in the project?

135 flats across two independent towers — Block A and Block B — each with stilt parking and five floors.

Is the project approved?

Yes. SV Garudadri is HMDA approved (No. 004088/BP/HMDA/GHT/2023) and registered with TS RERA (No. P02200007683).

What is the price?

Prices depend on the block, facing and floor. Share your details and our team will send the current price sheet. Car parking, registration charges and GST are extra.

Is a home loan available?

Yes. The project is financed by IIFL Home Finance Ltd., and our team will assist you with home-loan options.
